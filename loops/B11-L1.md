### Seed stores

Counts are quota-aware on purpose: 50 over 6 averages ~8, which overflows the
free tier's 10-item cap and would birth a store at 13/10 that B13 then refuses to
edit. Slice order below is the `row_number` order.

|   # | Store                  | Package | Items | rn range | Login                    |
| --: | ---------------------- | ------- | ----: | -------- | ------------------------ |
|   1 | ร้านป้าหมวย สยาม       | premium |    18 | 1–18     | `paamuay@store.test`     |
|   2 | Chic Corner ทองหล่อ    | basic   |    12 | 19–30    | `chiccorner@store.test`  |
|   3 | ห้องเสื้อ พี่สม อารีย์ | basic   |     9 | 31–39    | `pheesom@store.test`     |
|   4 | ตู้เสื้อผ้าน้องเมย์    | free    |     5 | 40–44    | `nongmay@store.test`     |
|   5 | Lila Vintage เอกมัย    | free    |     4 | 45–48    | `lilavintage@store.test` |
|   6 | ร้านลุงชาติ จตุจักร    | free    |     2 | 49–50    | `lungchat@store.test`    |

Dev PIN for all six: `246810`. `.test` is an RFC 2606 reserved TLD — unroutable,
which is why `email_confirm: true` is safe.

**No phone numbers in seed data** — any plausible Thai number could belong to a
real person. Seeds carry `address` + `contact_line` instead, which still
satisfies the "at least one contact channel" rule. Map URLs are generic
`google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=…` links, not fabricated place IDs.
